# Acme Billing Policy (doc_id: billing_policy)

## §1 Subscriptions
Subscriptions are charged automatically at the start of each billing cycle regardless of account usage or transaction volume. An active subscription is billed on its schedule until explicitly cancelled.

## §2 Zero-Transaction Accounts
An account that records zero transactions during a billing period but holds an active subscription is still charged the full subscription fee at the next billing cycle. Usage volume does not affect subscription billing. The subscription remains active and all subscription benefits are retained. The billing date is unaffected by the absence of transactions.

## §3 Payment Retry (Dunning)
If a subscription charge fails, the standard payment retry policy applies: an immediate retry, followed by retries on day 3 and day 7. Access is retained during a 14-day grace period before suspension.

## §4 Proration
Mid-cycle upgrades are prorated immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle with no proration credit.
