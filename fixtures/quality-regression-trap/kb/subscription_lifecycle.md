# Subscription Lifecycle (doc_id: subscription_lifecycle)

## States
active → past_due → suspended → cancelled

## Active
An active subscription entitles the account to all plan benefits. Billing continues on schedule. Transaction activity (or lack thereof) does not change the subscription state or the amount charged.

## Benefits Retention
Benefits are tied to subscription state, not usage. An active subscription with zero transactions retains full benefits.

## Billing Date
The billing date is fixed at the subscription start day and does not shift due to usage patterns or zero-transaction periods.
